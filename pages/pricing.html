01How do I get started?
Sign up, set up your company profile, and post your first job — completely free. You can then upgrade to the Growth or Scale plan when you need AI-assisted sourcing, advanced features, or a dedicated hiring workflow.
02What is included in the Free plan?
The Free plan includes up to 5 job listings per month, limited candidate database access, Smart Candidate Cards, basic applicant management, email notifications, and standard support.
03What is included in the Growth plan (₹5,999/month)?
Growth includes up to 50 job listings per month, AI-powered candidate matching, resume screening, ATS tools, detailed candidate cards, collaboration for up to 5 team members, reports, and priority support.
04What is included in the Scale plan (₹9,999/month)?
Scale includes up to 200 job listings per month, advanced AI matching, full ATS pipeline management, analytics, custom workflows, collaboration for up to 20 team members, and a dedicated account manager.
05Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?
Yes. You can choose the plan that suits your hiring requirements and change your plan as your needs evolve. Contact support for help.
06Is there a long-term contract?
Our plans are designed to be flexible, without a long-term commitment. Check your selected plan's terms before subscribing.
07What happens to my data if I cancel?
Please review our data retention policy or contact support to understand how your company and candidate data will be handled after cancellation.
08Do you offer a refund?
Refund eligibility depends on the applicable subscription terms. Contact support for assistance with a billing question.
09Do you integrate with other job portals or ATS?
Integration availability depends on the services supported by TalentBridge AI. Contact our team to discuss your existing hiring tools.
10How does AI candidate matching work?
AI-assisted matching can compare job requirements with candidate profiles to help recruiters discover relevant skills and experience. Recruiters should review candidates and make the final hiring decision.
